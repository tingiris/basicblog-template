Frontmatter fields:

```
---
title: "Title"
excerpt: "Short description"
coverImage: "/assets/blog/<slug>/cover.jpg" # optional
date: "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ"
author:
  name: Your Name
  picture: "/assets/blog/authors/you.png"
ogImage:
  url: "/assets/blog/<slug>/cover.jpg"
---
```

Drafts: name files `draft_<slug>.md` to hide them in production.
